Our Story

Food made with heart

From a small family kitchen in Andhra Pradesh to a table full of regulars — here's how Rangoli came to be.

Rangoli was created with one simple idea: bring people together through the bold, fiery flavours of Andhra cooking we grew up loving.

Our kitchen combines traditional Andhra recipes with fresh ingredients and a contemporary dining experience. Every plate is prepared with attention to flavour, freshness and presentation.

Whether you are joining us for breakfast, an evening filter coffee, a family dinner or a special celebration, Rangoli is a place where good food and good conversations come together.

How We Got Here

A short history of Rangoli

2011

A family kitchen opens its doors

Rangoli started as a ten-table kitchen, serving filter coffee and pesarattu to the neighbourhood.

2016

The menu grows up

Rangoli specials joined the menu, bringing gongura curries and Guntur chilli favourites to the table.

2021

A dining room built for gathering

We redesigned the space around long tables and warm lighting, made for shared meals.

2026

Rangoli today

Still family-run, still cooking to order, now welcoming guests online for reservations too.
