Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes — walk-ins are always welcome, though reservations are recommended for weekends and evenings.

Is there parking available?

Yes, parking is available directly outside the restaurant.

Do you cater for private events?

We do. Reach out through the reservation form or by phone and our team will help plan your event.

Are vegetarian and Jain options available?

Yes, most dishes can be prepared vegetarian or Jain-style on request — just let your server know.
