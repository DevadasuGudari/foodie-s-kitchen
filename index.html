Our Story

Food made with heart

Rangoli was created with one simple idea: bring people together through the food we grew up loving.

Our kitchen combines traditional Indian recipes with fresh ingredients and a contemporary dining experience. Every plate is prepared with attention to flavour, freshness and presentation.

Whether you are joining us for breakfast, an evening chai, a family dinner or a special celebration, Rangoli is a place where good food and good conversations come together.
